Computer

Windows

Tacheo Topside for Windows 10 and 11

⬇ Download for Windows
  1. Unzip it somewhere you can write to, for example Documents.
  2. Run Tacheo Topside.exe. If Windows says it protected your PC: More info → Run anyway.
  3. After that Topside finds and installs its own updates.

Microsoft Store version: coming soon.

Mac

Tacheo Topside for macOS (Tacheo pages; CartoTable is Windows-only for now)

⬇ Apple silicon (M1 and newer) ⬇ Intel Mac
  1. Not sure which? Apple menu → About This Mac: "Chip: Apple" is Apple silicon.
  2. Unzip, then the first time right-click → Open, and Open again.

Phone

Android

Topside phone app

⬇ Download for Android
  1. Open the download and tap Install. The first time, allow your browser to install apps.
  2. If Play Protect warns about an unknown developer: More details → Install anyway.
  3. Opened this from a message app? Use its menu → Open in Chrome first.

iPhone and iPad

Topside phone app (home-screen app)

⬇ Download for iPhone / iPad
  1. Open this page in Safari. Tap Allow.
  2. Settings → Profile Downloaded → Install. Topside appears on the home screen.
  3. Or in Safari: Share → Add to Home Screen.

The phone app shows your caves' line plots with no signal, leads and notes, sends photos of book pages and sketches to your computer, and connects to Topside on your computer. Updating a Tacheo, calibration reports and diagnostics need Topside on a computer. Open the phone app in this browser.

Tacheo firmware and manuals

Unit firmware and the instruction manuals come with Topside, so there's nothing separate to download:

  • Firmware: plug the Tacheo in with Topside open, then Updates and Upgrades → Tacheo. Topside keeps the right firmware for each kind of unit.
  • Manuals: Topside's Manuals page opens and prints them.
