Tacheo Topside - Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

Tacheo Topside ("Topside") is made by Phillip M. Sullivan. This policy covers the Windows program (including the Microsoft Store version), the Mac program and the Topside phone app.

What Topside keeps

Everything Topside makes or downloads - shot downloads, survey files, calibration records, backups, settings and reports - stays on your own computer (in the Tacheo Topside folder, or Documents\Tacheo Topside for the Microsoft Store version) and your own phone. Topside has no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not sell or share anything about you.

When Topside uses the internet

Only for the following. The ones marked * ask you first.

  • Checking for updates (the downloaded version): Topside reads a small version file from github.com, or Google Drive on a Mac. Nothing about you is sent. The Microsoft Store version is updated by the Store.
  • Elevation*: the elevation service is sent a rectangle widened to at least 1 km round the cave and snapped to a half-kilometre grid - never a station position.
  • Satellite and map imagery*: the map server is sent the map tiles covering what is on screen, so it can tell roughly which area you are looking at. No survey data is sent.
  • Geology*: macrostrat.org is sent a grid of 25 points spread over your elevation model, not the cave's stations.
  • Support reports and suggestions: sent only when you press Send, and they contain only what the report window shows, including any contact details you type in.

The phone app and Phone link

Phone link connects your phone directly to Topside on your own computer, on your own network (or through Tailscale, if you set that up). Caves, photos and notes go between your phone and your computer only. The phone app keeps its caves and photos on the phone until you remove them; photos are removed from the phone once your computer has them. The phone app uses the camera only when you tap Scan or Take photos.

Survey and sketch keeps its surveys on the phone until you delete them, and sends them only to your own computer when you tap Send. The Android app uses Bluetooth only to find and talk to your survey instrument (a Tacheo or SAP6). On Android 12 and newer it asks for Nearby devices, marked as not used for location; on Android 11 and older Android requires the location permission for any Bluetooth search.

The phone's location (GPS) is read only while you have Show my location on, or while you are taking a GPS point (a GPS point, or the GPS at a survey's first station). Points you keep stay on the phone until you delete them, and go only to your own computer when you send them. The location is never sent anywhere else. Online map pictures for the area on screen are fetched from the map providers you pick, as before.

Children

Topside is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
