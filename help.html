What the app does

The Topside phone app carries your caves from CartoTable (the cave survey and map side of Tacheo Topside) on your phone. It:

  • shows the line plot in plan, profile and 3-D, over map imagery and the ground's shape, with no signal needed once a cave is on the phone;
  • finds where you are by station, the quickest way out, and how far it is between two stations;
  • lists leads nearest first, with their notes, and the trip books and trip reports;
  • plans a trip by travel time;
  • sends photos of survey book pages and sketches to your computer, where Topside reads the data pages into survey data;
  • connects to Topside on your computer, over your Wi-Fi or from anywhere through Tailscale.
The app doesn't connect to a Tacheo. Updating a unit's firmware, calibration reports and diagnostics need Topside on a computer.

Install it

Android

  1. Open the downloads page in Chrome and tap Download for Android.
  2. Open the download and tap Install. The first time, Android asks to let your browser install apps: allow it.
  3. If Play Protect says it doesn't know the developer: More details → Install anyway.

Opened the link inside Messenger or another message app? Use that app's ⋮ menu → Open in Chrome first. If you installed the web copy from Chrome earlier, it still works; the app from the download adds the link to your computer.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open the downloads page in Safari and tap Download for iPhone / iPad, then Allow.
  2. Open Settings → Profile Downloaded → Install. Topside appears on the home screen.

Or in Safari: Share → Add to Home Screen.

The first screen and Home

The first time, the app asks where you are: Topside (planning, maps, books and exports, normal colours) or In the cave (a dark screen, big buttons, only what you need underground). You can switch any time.

Home has five tiles:

My cavesEvery cave on this phone. Tap one to open it.
Add a caveFrom a .ctcave file, or straight from Topside on your computer.
Data photosPhotograph book pages and sketches and send them to your computer.
My computerPair with Topside on your computer, see its caves and send it files.
In the caveSwitch to the dark, big-button mode (or back to Topside mode).

Get a cave onto the phone

From your computer (easiest)

  1. Pair the phone once - see Connect to your computer.
  2. Add a cave → Caves on your computer, and pick the cave. Topside makes a fresh copy with its map layers and sends it over.

From a file

  1. On the computer, in CartoTable: Send to phone (File menu or the Exports page). It saves a .ctcave file to the CartoTable caves folder in your Google Drive, OneDrive, iCloud or Dropbox, and asks whether to include true coordinates.
  2. On the phone: Add a cave → Open a cave file (.ctcave) and pick it from Drive, Files, a message or your downloads. On Android you can also open or share a .ctcave straight to Topside.

Every cave you open stays on the phone and works with no signal. Remove one from My caves.

Connect to your computer

  1. On the computer, open Tacheo Topside and pick Connect a phone on its first screen. It shows a QR code and a 6-digit code.
  2. On the phone: My computer → Connect to my computer, then Scan the QR code (or Type the code instead). The code works for 15 minutes and pairs one phone.
  3. The phone and computer need the same Wi-Fi the first time. Topside has to be open on the computer (it can start with Windows - tick that in Connect a phone).

Once paired, My computer shows the caves Topside knows, runs Check and Reckon on the computer and shows the findings, takes files or a note from the phone, and Use its screen shows the computer's Topside window on the phone to use with taps and typing.

Away from home: install Tailscale on the computer and the phone and sign in to the same account on both. The app then reaches your computer from anywhere. My computer → Tailscale help walks through it. Forget this computer unpairs.

Data photos

  1. Data photos on Home. Pick the cave and the trip.
  2. Choose what you're photographing: Book pages, Sketches or Other.
  3. Take the photos with the app's camera (the light can be turned on), or choose them from the phone - several at once is fine.
  4. They're sent to your computer as soon as it can be reached, into Documents\Topside\From phone\day\cave - trip. Photos taken in the cave wait on the phone until there's signal, and nothing is removed from the phone until the computer has it.

On the computer, CartoTable reads the book pages into survey data (Read this page), sorts pages into trips and places the sketches. Uncertain numbers are marked for you to check against the photo.

For the best reading: the page flat and filling the frame, even light, no shadow of the phone across it, and the photo sharp.

Looking at a cave

  • Move: drag. Zoom: pinch. Turn the plan: twist with two fingers; it turns round the station you tapped.
  • Plan / Profile / 3-D at the top switches the view. In 3-D, drag to turn it.
  • Tap a station to see how far in it is (travel time and footage), its depth, elevation, how sure its position is (the 95 % circle), its notes and lead, and the trips that surveyed it.
  • Colour the line plot by travel time from the nearest entrance, by depth, by how precise the survey is, or by trip.
  • Show the way in / out draws the quickest route from the nearest entrance as amber dashes.

The tabs (Topside mode)

StationThe tapped station's details, and Plan a trip here.
LeadsLeads nearest first, with their notes and how to get there. Show it on the plot.
Trip planPick a start and a destination: travel time by how you're moving (walking, stooping, crawling, belly crawl, breakdown, climbing, on rope), leads on the way, and a brief to share.
BooksEvery trip's book and trip report, sorted as filed, newest, longest or weakest.
LayersMap imagery, the ground's shading and contours, sinks, drafted maps, station names and prefixes. Save for offline keeps the imagery on the phone.
ExportGPX, KML, GeoJSON, CSV, a picture of the map (with a world file), and the elevation.
AboutThe cave's summary and this app's version.

In the cave mode

A dark screen with big buttons that saves the battery. Its tabs:

HerePick the station you're at: how far in you are, the depth, what's near.
Way outThe quickest way to the nearest entrance, step by step, and the time it takes.
Two stationsDistance and travel time between any two stations.
Trip planThe trip you planned, with its leads.
LeadsLeads nearest to where you are.
BooksTrip books and reports for checking an old survey on the spot.

With no signal

Everything about a cave on the phone works underground: the plot, stations, the way out, leads, books, trip plans and exports. Map imagery shows where it was saved with Save for offline (Layers). Data photos wait on the phone and go to the computer later. Connecting to your computer needs Wi-Fi or Tailscale.

When something's wrong

There's no download button

The app hides it once it's installed. Inside a message app's browser, use its menu → Open in Chrome (or Safari). The downloads page always has every download.

It can't find my computer

  • Topside is open on the computer and Connect a phone has been set up once.
  • The phone is on the same Wi-Fi - or Tailscale is running on both.
  • Windows asked whether to allow Topside on networks: allow it on private networks.
  • Still not: My computer → Forget this computer, then pair again with a new code.

Android won't install it

Allow the browser to install apps when asked (Settings → Apps → your browser → Install unknown apps), and choose Install anyway if Play Protect asks.

A cave doesn't open

Make a fresh copy on the computer (Send to phone, or Add a cave → Caves on your computer). Caves made by an older Topside open, but a new copy brings the newest layers.

A newer version is out

The app says so at the top of Home. On Android, download it from the downloads page and install over the old one - your caves stay.
